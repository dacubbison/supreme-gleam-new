About Supreme Gleam Detailing

Mobile Auto Detailing Experts Serving The Woodlands, Kingwood & Montgomery County TX

Our Mission

Supreme Gleam Detailing is a premier mobile car detailing service founded in 2025. We bring a dream-like shine to vehicles across The Woodlands, Kingwood, and the greater Montgomery County area.

We focus on personalized, high-quality work using state-of-the-art equipment — high-pressure washers for safe rinses, professional polishers for precision paint correction, and premium ceramic coatings that protect your vehicle for up to 3 years.

At Supreme Gleam, customer satisfaction always comes first. We use eco-conscious methods that respect Texas’s water conservation efforts while delivering results that rival professional shops — all from the comfort of your driveway.

Meet the Owner

I’m David, a detailing enthusiast with a deep passion for automotive care and craftsmanship. Each project is treated like my own — with dedication to excellence and attention to every detail.
